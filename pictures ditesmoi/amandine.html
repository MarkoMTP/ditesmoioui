Experts in beautiful events

Dites Moi Oui is an event planning company based in the South of France, producing fabulous weddings and luxurious events in the most sought-after destinations. From the most romantic and elegant wedding in our magnificent Provence, to a beach party on a private beach in Corsica, a glamorous celebration in Marrakech, or a picturesque wedding in Puglia, Italy, we create bespoke events for you.

Our
commitments

  • Our experience Géraldine founded the company in 2005 and has been managing event production ever since. Her sister, Amandine, joined her in 2012.
  • A wide range of splendid locations We are constantly searching for new and beautiful places.
  • Attentive to detail We firmly believe that details are crucial to the success of an event, and we are not afraid to spend hours on a small detail that can make all the difference.
  • Kindly devoted Our goal is to develop much more than just a professional relationship with you. We look forward to becoming almost like a member of your family for a time. We are there for you, whatever it takes.
  • Curious about each culture We love learning about new cultures, and that's one of the reasons why we enjoy organizing "destination weddings," among other things.

Every event begins with a story

Over the coming months, we are proud to become your confidantes on all matters. We will listen attentively, taking note of your personalities to create a spectacular event.
