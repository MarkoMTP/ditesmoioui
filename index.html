The Sisters

Meet Géraldine & Amandine

Géraldine founded Dites Moi Oui in 2005, and her sister Amandine joined her as partner in 2012. From our offices in Marseille, we plan weddings and private celebrations across Provence, the Côte d'Azur and far beyond, always together and always personally.

We take on a limited number of celebrations each year, so that every couple has our full attention. Over the months of planning, we become more than your planners: we become your confidantes, from the very first call to the last dance.
